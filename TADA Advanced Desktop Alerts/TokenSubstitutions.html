Simple Substitutions

{name}
The name of the person who: dropped bits, followed the channel, gifted a subscription, hosted the channel, subscribed to the channel, or talked in chat. If this is an anonymous event, then "An Anonymous Gifter" and "An Anonymous Bit Dropper" will be substituted instead.
Thank you, {name}, for that subscription!
Thank you, Invariel, for that subscription!
{amount}
The amount of bits dropped, subscriptions gifted at once, users who came along with the host, or months subscribed for.
Thank you for that very generous {amount} bit drop!
Thank you for that very generous 573 bit drop!
{giftee}
The name of the person receiving a gift subscription, or in the case of a mass gift, the list of people gifted to.
Welcome {giftee} to the club, thanks to a gifted subscription!
Welcome LeiRavni to the club, thanks to a gifted subscription!
{s}
If {amount} is not 1, then print s.
Thank you for subscribing for {amount} month{s}.
Thank you for subscribing for 1 month.
Thank you for subscribing for 3 months.
{es}
If {amount} is not 1, then print es.
A donation of {amount} bit{s}? That's like {amount} whole splash{es}! Making waves!
A donation of 1 bit? That's like 1 whole splash! Making waves!
A donation of 573 bits? That's like 573 whole splashes! Making waves!
{plan}
The current subscription plan for the user: Unsubscribed, Tier One, Tier Two, or Tier Three. Note that all Twitch Prime subscribers have the Tier One plan.
Thank you, {name} ({plan}), for those {amount} bits.
Thank you, Invariel (Unsubscribed), for those 250 bits.
Thank you, Invariel (Tier One), for those 250 bits.
Thank you, Invariel (Tier Two), for those 250 bits.
Thank you, Invariel (Tier Three), for those 250 bits.
{tier}
The tier that the user subscribed at: Twitch Prime, Tier One, Tier Two, or Tier Three.
Thank you, {name}, for that {tier} subscription.
Thank you, Invariel, for that Twitch Prime subscription.
Thank you, Invariel, for that Tier One subscription.
Thank you, Invariel, for that Tier Two subscription.
Thank you, Invariel, for that Tier Three subscription.
{tiername}
The customized name for the tier that was subscribed at.
Thank you, {name}, for your support as a {tiername}.
Thank you, Invariel, for your support as a Level 1 White Mage.
Thank you, Invariel, for your support as a Level 1 Black Mage.
Thank you, Invariel, for your support as a Level 1 Red Mage.

More Complex Substitutions

[a|b]
If {amount} is 1, then print a. Otherwise, print b.
Your {amount} month{s} of support [is|are] appreciated.
Your 1 month of support is appreciated.
Your 5 months of support are appreciated.
<a|b|c|d|e>
If the user is not subscribed, a. If the user is subscribed, then use b for a Twitch Prime subscriber, c for a Tier One subscriber, d for a Tier Two subscriber, or e for a Tier Three subscriber.
I am <appreciative of|thankful for|grateful for|very grateful for|humbled by> your continued support.
I am appreciative of your continued support.
I am thankful for your continued support.
I am grateful foryour continued support.
I am very grateful for your continued support.
I am humbled by your continued support.
